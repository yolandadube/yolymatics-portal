Concept 01

TEMPERATURE

Temperature is a measure of how fast the particles inside a substance are moving.

The faster the particles vibrate and move → the higher the temperature.

Temperature does not tell us how much energy there is — only how intense the particle motion is.

Faster = Hotter →

Concept 02

THERMAL
ENERGY

Thermal energy is the total energy of all the moving particles inside a substance.

A large cold object can have more thermal energy than a small hot one — because it has far more particles.

It lives inside every atom, in the form of kinetic energy — the energy of movement.

Heat Transfer 01

CONDUCTION

Conduction is heat transfer through direct contact.

Hot, fast-moving particles bump into cooler, slower neighbours — passing on their energy like a chain reaction.

It happens mainly in solids, especially metals.

Heat Transfer 02

CONVECTION

Convection is heat transfer through the movement of fluids (liquids or gases).

Hot fluid rises (it's less dense) while cool fluid sinks — creating a continuous convection current.

This is how your radiator heats a room, and how ocean currents flow!

Heat Transfer 03

RADIATION

Radiation is heat transfer through electromagnetic waves — it needs no medium at all.

This is how the Sun's heat crosses 150 million km of empty space to reach Earth.

All objects emit thermal radiation. Hotter objects emit more — and that's why you feel warmth from a fire without touching it.
