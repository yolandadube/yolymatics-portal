06.01 Comprehensive Science II : Biotechnology Assignment

Benefits, Risks, and Impacts of Biotechnology

Today’s Activity: Take a Stand!

You have learned about how biotechnology can help people, but also how it can be risky. Now it is your turn to choose a side and explain your thinking clearly.

Your task: Choose ONE type of biotechnology and argue for either its benefits or its risks.
Genetic Engineering Cloning Artificial Selection

Step 1: Choose and Describe Your Biotechnology

First, choose one of the three options below and write a 1–2 paragraph summary explaining what it is and how it works.

Option A: Genetic Engineering

  • Changing or adding genes in an organism’s DNA.
  • Can give organisms new traits, such as pest resistance or faster growth.

Option B: Cloning

  • Making a new organism that is genetically identical to another one.
  • Can be done with plants, animals, and even individual cells.

Option C: Artificial Selection

  • Humans choose which plants or animals reproduce to get desired traits.
  • Used for crops, pets, and farm animals over many generations.

Step 2: Decide — Are You For It or Against It?

Next, decide if you are for your chosen biotechnology or against it. Your paragraphs must:

1 State Your Position

Clearly say if you support or oppose this type of biotechnology.

Example: “I support genetic engineering because it can help treat serious diseases.”

2 Explain Your Evidence

Use information from your lessons or research to support your position.

Start sentences with “Scientists have found…”, “Research shows…”, or “In our lesson, we learned that…”.

3 Connect to Individual, Society, Environment

Show how your biotechnology affects people, communities, and the natural world.

4 Write in Complete Sentences

Use full sentences and your own words. Check spelling and punctuation.

Step 3: Checklist – What Your Argument Must Include

Make sure your final work includes ALL of these parts:

Biotechnology Argument Checklist
Requirement Details Space for Your Notes
Description Describe your chosen biotechnology in your own words. ✔ Did I explain what it is?
Individual Include one benefit or one risk for an individual person or organism. ✔ Did I give a clear example?
Society Include one benefit or one risk for society (a group, community, or country). ✔ Did I explain how it affects groups of people?
Environment Include one benefit or one risk for the environment (ecosystems, plants, animals, etc.). ✔ Did I connect it to nature?
Picture Add a picture that matches your topic (drawn, photo, or stock image). ✔ Did I choose or draw a picture?
1–2 Paragraphs Write one or two well-organized paragraphs including all parts above. ✔ Did I revise and proofread?

Step 4: Choose How You Will Present Your Work

Remember the Picture!

Your assignment must include at least one picture that connects to your topic.

  • Draw your own illustration and upload or attach it.
  • Take your own photo in nature or of a model you create.
  • Use a school-appropriate stock image.

Be Creative – Choose a Format

You may present your 1–2 paragraphs and picture in one of these formats:

Brochure

Design a colorful brochure with headings, short sections, and your picture.

Letter

Write a letter to a friend, family member, or leader explaining your position.

Presentation

Create slides using software (PowerPoint, Google Slides, etc.).

Other (Ask First)

If you have another idea or software, check with your instructor for approval.
