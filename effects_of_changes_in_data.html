07 / 19Key Concept

⭐ The Most Important Idea in This Lesson

Median vs Mean — Which One to Choose?

📊

Mean

The mean IS affected by outliers. Adding one extreme value can drastically change the average, making it an unfair representation of the rest of the data.

⚠️ Use with caution when outliers exist!

📍

Median

The median is resistant to outliers. It only cares about the middle position in sorted data. Extreme values barely move it.

✅ Better choice when data has outliers!

Rule of thumb: If your data has an outlier, use the median as your measure of center.
It gives a more honest picture of what's typical in the data set.

📊 Why the big difference?

A grade of 55 is an outlier — it's far below all her other grades. Outliers have a large impact on the mean and range.

A grade of 88 is close to her existing average, so it barely changes anything.

🎓 Verdict: Meena should STUDY!

Mathematical reason: When an outlier is added, the mean is significantly affected because it uses ALL values in calculation. The median only cares about the middle position, so it moved less — but even it was impacted by the very low grade of 55.

12 / 19Patterns

🔢 Think Like a Mathematician

Patterns in How Data Changes

Notice these patterns as you study more examples — they will help you predict what will happen before you even calculate!

Type of ChangeImpact on Mean & RangeImpact on Median & IQR
Add a value larger than the mean (outlier) Mean ↑ significantly · Range ↑ drastically Median barely changes · IQR slightly ↑
Add a value equal to the mean Mean stays the same Median may shift slightly · IQR unchanged
Add a value smaller than the mean (outlier) Mean ↓ significantly · Range ↑ drastically Median barely changes · IQR slightly ↑
Increase an existing value above the mean Mean ↑ · Range may ↑ Median may ↑ or stay same
Remove a value close to the mean Mean changes slightly Median shifts, Range & IQR small change
Remove an outlier Mean changes significantly · Range ↓ drastically Median stays near same · IQR ↓
🔑 Golden Rule: The mean and range are most affected by extreme values (outliers). They are the most sensitive measures.
🛡️ Resistant Measures: The median and IQR are much more resistant to outliers and extreme changes.

19 / 19Summary

🎓 Lesson Summary

Effects of Changes in Data — Key Takeaways

📊

Mean & Range

These are most sensitive to changes. Adding an outlier causes them to change significantly. The mean uses all values, so extreme values have a large pull.

📍

Median & IQR

These are resistant to outliers. They barely change when extreme values are added or removed. They represent the "typical" data more honestly.

🎯

When to Use Each

Use median when data has outliers. Use mean when data is evenly spread. Always check for outliers before choosing your measure!

🔑 The Golden Rule

If you see an outlier in your data — a value far away from the others — use the median instead of the mean to represent what's typical. The median tells the honest story!
